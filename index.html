🍜 Your Standard Bowl

354 ml broth + 60 ml tare

Tare salt concentration: 12,8% NaCl

With unsalted broth, this gives approximately: 1,86% final NaCl

This is the reference ratio used by the Ramen Lab calculator.

📐 How it works

The calculator treats salt percentage as the percentage of NaCl by mass.

For your standard bowl:
354 g broth + 60 g tare = 414 g total

60 × 12,8% = 7,68 g NaCl

7,68 ÷ 414 × 100 = 1,86%

Decimal commas are supported: 12,8 = 12.8.
